        Where this comes from
        • Voting rules · each state's own election office, researched Oct 26, 2026. Always confirm with your state, since things can change.
        • What's on the ballot · senate.gov and state election offices. Candidates' own words · Same Question, checked against the debate audio.
        • Rent · Zillow Observed Rent Index, updated monthly.
        • Jobs and prices · U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (state unemployment; consumer prices by region).
        • Health, benefits, taxes, holidays · HealthCare.gov, Medicare.gov, CMS, Social Security, IRS, OPM.
        • Weather alerts · National Weather Service. Disaster declarations · OpenFEMA. Weather and air · Open-Meteo.
        • Finding your place · Zippopotam.us and the FCC Area API.
